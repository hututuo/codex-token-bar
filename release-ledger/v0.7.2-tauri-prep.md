## Included Follow-ups After Initial v0.7.2 Prep

- Codex CLI discovery no longer depends on a single App name. The Tauri quota reader supports explicit `CODEX_CLI_PATH`, LaunchServices candidates, bounded `/Applications` and `~/Applications` scans, known compatibility candidates, Homebrew, and PATH.
- App-bundle candidates, including LaunchServices and known-name candidates, must carry `CFBundleIdentifier = com.openai.codex`; wrong, missing, or invalid bundle metadata is rejected. Standalone explicit/Homebrew/PATH CLI candidates remain supported.
- The maintained Codex Desktop sidebar patch helper now uses explicit-path and bundle-identity-based App discovery rather than a fixed App name.
- Same-source trusted usage summaries now remain visible during ordinary session appends while one background aggregate refresh runs. Live-rate and floating reads use the lightweight in-memory scope and no longer rescan the whole session tree on every tick.
