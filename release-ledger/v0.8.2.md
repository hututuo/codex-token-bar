## Scope

v0.8.2 replaces the v0.8.0 bounded-history guard with unlimited exact SQLite indexes on
both platforms. The index supports byte-level append resume, changed-file rebuilds,
parallel cold staging, recoverable Tauri build generations, durable reuse across app
updates, and compact aggregate reuse for active refreshes. It stores numeric accounting,
timestamps, source identity, and offsets rather than conversation text.
